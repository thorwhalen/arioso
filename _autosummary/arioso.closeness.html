arioso.closeness#

Is a take a cover of its source? Lyric recall when there are words, melody rank otherwise.

Measured on the 2026-11-10 folder job (arioso#69, #81): six Suno upload-cover takes of three home-recorded children’s songs, scored against those three sources and three unrelated songs by the same family. arioso.analysis.melody_match() alone cannot answer “is this take a cover of that song”: a take’s score against its own source (0.57 to 0.71 on the mix) sits inside the range of its scores against unrelated songs (up to 0.66), so no fixed threshold separates them. Two signals did:

  • lyric recall (the source’s words found, in order, in the take’s words): 0.60 to 0.94 against the true source, at most 0.15 against any other, for every take with words;

  • melody rank: for the instrumental takes, the true source had the highest melody_match of all six songs on the mix (by 0.05 to 0.11), though not in absolute terms.

So cover_closeness() reports both and gives a verdict from the one that applies: the words when the source and the take both have them, else the melody’s rank among references (other songs: the rest of the folder serves), else no verdict (“unclear”).

>>> lyric_recall("stinky poo where are you", "oh stinky poo stinky poo where are you now")
1.0
>>> lyric_recall("stinky poo where are you", "climbing up the mountain")
0.0
>>> lyric_recall("", "anything") is None
True

arioso.closeness.cover_closeness(source, takes, *, references=(), transcribe='auto', source_text=None, melody=None, load=None, min_words=12)[source]#

How close each take is to source, and whether it is a cover of it.

Matches cover_folder’s assess keyword: (source_path, take_paths) -> dict; give it references with functools.partial(cover_closeness, references=[...]).

Parameters:

  • source – The source song’s audio file.

  • takes (Iterable) – The takes’ audio files.

  • references (Iterable) – Other songs’ audio files, for the melody’s rank (the source itself, if listed, is skipped). Without them a take with no words gets no verdict.

  • transcribe (Union[Callable, str, None]) – (path) -> text; the default is arioso.cover.vocal_lyrics() (the vocal stem, transcribed: pip install arioso[cover]); None skips the words.

  • source_text (Optional[str]) – The source’s words, when already known (it is then not transcribed).

  • melody (Optional[Callable]) – (take, source, sr) -> float, default arioso.analysis.melody_match().

  • load (Optional[Callable]) – (path) -> mono array at DFLT_SR; default librosa.load.

  • min_words (int) – The fewest words each side needs for the words to decide.

Returns:

name, “source_words”: n, “references”: [names], “takes”: {name: {“lyric_recall”, “take_words”, “melody”, “melody_rank”, “verdict”, “why”}}}``.

Return type:

dict
